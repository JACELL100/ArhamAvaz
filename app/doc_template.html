1. Executive Summary & Core Value Proposition

ArhamAawaaz is a specialized, AI-driven outbound voice calling and customer engagement platform built specifically for Insurance Advisors, Independent Brokers, and Agencies across India and global markets.

The Industry Challenge
Insurance advisors spend over 65% of their daily working hours making manual follow-up calls for policy renewals, lead qualification, and rollover reminders. With high call drop rates and language barriers, advisors lose high-value renewals to lapses.

ArhamAawaaz solves this by deploying conversational AI agents capable of speaking natural Hindi, English, and Hinglish. The platform automates mass dialing, follows customizable insurance script flows, captures customer intent, and logs structured outcomes—saving advisors up to 120+ hours per month.

Key Differentiators & Platform Capabilities

AI Engine Multilingual Voice Bots

Human-sounding conversational AI agent ("Deepali") capable of fluidly speaking Hindi, English, and Hinglish with contextual insurance knowledge.

Data Upload Bulk Excel & CSV Import

Seamlessly import customer lists from Excel (.xlsx/.xls) or CSV files with auto-detection of Name, Phone, and Insurance Category fields.

Finances Dual-Currency Wallet

Integrated billing with Razorpay (INR + GST) and Dodo Payments (USD Merchant of Record) supporting instant wallet top-ups.

Cross-Platform Web & Android App

Built using React 19 + Vite for web browsers and Capacitor JS for native Android deployment on mobile devices.

2. Detailed Module & Application Architecture

The ArhamAawaaz interface is organized into five core functional modules accessible via a persistent navigation sidebar (desktop) or tabbar (mobile):

Module 1: Dashboard & Analytics (`Home.jsx`)

Provides real-time visibility into overall calling operations and performance metrics:

  • Total Calls Placed: Total count of automated calls dispatched through the system.
  • Answer Rate & Duration: Percentage of successfully connected calls and total talk time logged.
  • Call Queue Preview: Real-time status of pending contacts waiting to be dialed.
  • Recent Executions List: Live feed displaying completed calls, customer names, call duration, and AI-generated summary tags.

Module 2: Outbound Call Center (`CallPage.jsx`)

The core operational engine where insurance advisors queue and trigger automated calling campaigns:

  • Bulk Import Engine (`contactsFile.js`): Parses Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV spreadsheets using the xlsx library. Automatically standardizes columns for Name, Phone Number, and Policy Type.
  • Manual Lead Entry: Quick form for single-contact immediate calling.
  • Campaign Customization: Select target language (Hindi, English, Hinglish), script domain (Health, Life, Motor), and policy stage (New Policy, Renewal, Rollover/Port).
  • Live State Polling: Tracks real-time status transitions: queued → initiated → in-progress → answered / no-answer.

Module 3: Call Responses & Transcripts (`Responses.jsx`)

Comprehensive repository of call outputs, transcripts, and intelligence:

  • Dual-Speaker Transcript View: Line-by-line dialogue history between the AI Advisor ("Deepali") and the Customer, supporting Hindi Devanagari script rendering.
  • AI Executive Summary: Automated extraction of key customer responses (e.g., "Customer requested quote for 10L sum insured, call back at 5 PM").
  • Status Badges: Visual indicators for Answered, Missed, Busy, and Failed calls.
  • Audio Playback: Direct link/audio player for recorded call audio when enabled.

3. Insurance Domains & Pre-Built Script Engine

ArhamAawaaz comes out-of-the-box with tailored conversational scripts designed for insurance regulations, objection handling, and compliance requirement in India.

Supported Insurance Domains

Domain Policy Stages Key Dialogue & Objection Focus
Health Insurance New, Renewal, Port Sum insured evaluation, pre-existing disease disclosure, waiting-period credit retention, room rent limits, cashless network hospital explanation.
Life Insurance New, Renewal, Upgrade Term plan vs endowment comparison, Section 80C tax benefits, riders (accidental/critical illness), grace period reminders, auto-debit setup.
Motor Insurance New, Renewal, Rollover Mandatory third-party cover, Zero Depreciation add-ons, Engine Protection, No Claim Bonus (NCB) transfer during rollover, instant policy issuance.

Sample Script Structure (Health Policy Renewal - Hindi/Hinglish)

AI Persona: Deepali (ArhamAawaaz Advisor)
Greeting: "Namaste Priya ji, main ArhamAawaaz se Deepali bol rahi hoon. Aapki Health Policy Renewal ke regarding 2 minute baat kar sakte hain?"
Step 1 (Expiry Check): "Aapki policy agle hafte expire ho rahi hai. Expiry se pehle renew karne se aapka No Claim Bonus aur Waiting Period credit safe rahega."
Step 2 (Sum Insured Upgrade): "Kya aap kisi naye family member ko add karna chahte hain ya Sum Insured increase karna chahte hain?"
Step 3 (Outcome Capture): Confirm renewal willingness, preferred payment mode, and send payment link via SMS/WhatsApp.

Custom Script Editor (`SettingsPage.jsx`)

Advisors can customize the AI agent persona name, company branding, greeting templates, and prompt guidelines directly from the Settings tab without editing any code.

5. Book Demo Lead Engine & Landing System

The ArhamAawaaz landing page (`LandingPage.jsx`) features a dedicated conversion funnel tailored to onboard new insurance agencies and brokers.

Interactive "Book Demo" Feature (`BookDemoModal.jsx`)

Replacing static CTA buttons, the Book Demo modal enables visitors to request a 1-on-1 personalized walk-through:

  • Lead Capture Form: Collects Full Name, Work Email, Phone/WhatsApp Number, Target Insurance Segment (Health/Life/Motor), Preferred Date, and Time Slot.
  • Automated Email Trigger: Upon form submission, the modal formats all lead parameters and triggers an instant email notification (mailto:support@arhamavaz.com) to alert the sales and onboarding team.
  • Confirmation Screen: Displays an instant "Demo Request Received! 🎉" summary card displaying the booked date, time slot, and assigned advisor follow-up notice.
